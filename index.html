01 — The Incident

They Heard Her Singing.

At exactly 3:17 AM, every resident of Blackwood heard the same sound.

A woman singing somewhere beneath the town. Not outside. Not inside a house. Beneath them.

The first person who went looking never came back. Then another disappeared. Then another.

"The song stopped at 3:33 AM. That's when the knocking started."

Authorities sealed the old underground tunnels before sunrise. The official report called it a gas leak. Nobody believed them.
